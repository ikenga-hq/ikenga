Projects other people shared with you now appear in the project switcher (the title-row project chip and the ⌘P palette) under "Shared with you", not only in Settings → People & devices. Inside a shared project the chip shows its name with a "shared by …" marker and the switcher's first item is "Leave shared project"; picking one of your own projects from there leaves the share before switching.
